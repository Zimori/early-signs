# Early Signs (An Economical Warnings Study)

How reliable are economic indicators as early warning signals of recessions across different economic regimes?

## Project Overview
Data Science project exploring macroeconomic indicators (unemployment rate, inflation, consumer confidence, gdp growth, interest rate variations, ...) to help predict early signs of an economic recession. 


## Why it Matters
I believe identifying early warning signs of economic downturns ultimately helps mitigate their impact on businesses and society as a whole. Understanding when a crisis erupts eventually allows policymakers, businesses, individuals to make informed decisions and allocate resources in a more effective way. "Chance favors the prepared mind" - Louis Pasteur.
